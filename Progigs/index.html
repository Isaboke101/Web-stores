Who We Are

An Events Company
Built On Precision

Pro Gigs Audio Visual Limited is a Nairobi-based event management and audio visual company. We plan, design and execute events end to end — bringing together technical proficiency, creative direction and disciplined logistics so that nothing is left to chance.

From intimate corporate briefings to large-scale conferences and national brand activations, we work as an extension of your team, translating objectives into experiences that land with your audience.

  • Full in-house audio, lighting and staging inventory
  • Structured 3-tier risk management on every event
  • Vetted vendor and venue network across East Africa
  • Single point of contact from brief to debrief
