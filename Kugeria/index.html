The Person Behind Kūgeria

Why I Started Kūgeria

Kūgeria is Kikuyu for "to try, to attempt, to innovate." I started this agency on a simple belief: every bold business attempt deserves engineering that matches its ambition. Too many Kenyan businesses settle for templated websites that sit idle — I build digital systems that actively work as silent sales agents, around the clock.

Computer Science student at The Co-operative University of Kenya, active in the IEEE Student Branch
Full-stack developer working across Python, PHP, JavaScript, and AI/LLM integration
Every project is built custom — no page builders, no bloated templates, full ownership of your code
Based in Nairobi, working with clients across Kenya and beyond
