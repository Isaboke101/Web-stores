This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the requirements of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. By accessing or using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Personal Data

While using our service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, particularly to facilitate scheduling and conducting meetings with you. This includes:

  • First name and Last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical Address
  • Social Media Information (e.g., handles, profile links if you interact with our social channels)

Data Storage Mechanism

The personal information we collect (such as contact details submitted via forms to arrange meetings) is securely transmitted and stored within Google Sheets utilizing Google Apps Script.

Payment Information

Since users pay for our products and services, we collect necessary payment details. Please note that all payment transactions are processed securely, and we do not store sensitive payment card details directly on our servers or in our Google Sheets.

2. How We Use Your Information

Kūgeria Ltd uses the collected data for various purposes, including:

  • To facilitate meetings: Organizing, scheduling, and conducting consultations or service meetings with you.
  • To provide and maintain our products and services
  • To process your payments and manage your orders
  • To communicate with you, including sending order updates, customer support responses, and service-related notices
  • Marketing & Communications: We send emails to users who have explicitly opted-in to receive them. We plan to send advertisements and promotional materials in the future, and you will always have the right to opt-out or unsubscribe at any time.
  • To monitor the usage of our website and improve user experience.

3. Data Retention

We will retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Client and Meeting Data: We retain contact information used to facilitate meetings and manage active client relationships for the duration of our business relationship, and for up to three (3) years after our last interaction, for administrative and record-keeping purposes.
  • Marketing Data: If you have opted-in to our marketing emails, we will retain your email address until you request to unsubscribe or opt-out.
  • Financial Records: Transactional data related to payments will be kept for up to seven (7) years to comply with Kenyan tax and accounting legal obligations.

Once the retention period expires, your personal data stored in our Google Sheets and other systems will be securely deleted or anonymized.

4. Third-Party Tracking and Analytics

While we do not currently use web analytics or remarketing services, we are planning to implement Google Analytics and targeted/remarketing advertising services in the near future. Once active, these third-party services will use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website, analyze traffic, and serve tailored ads across the internet based on your past visits to our site. We will update this policy and provide relevant cookie consent options when these features are rolled out.

5. Children's Privacy

Our products, services, and website are not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers promptly.

6. Your Data Protection Rights (Kenya DPA, 2019)

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to be informed: You have the right to know how your data is being used (which is the purpose of this policy).
  • Right of access: You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure (Right to be forgotten): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data when there is no compelling reason for its continued processing.
  • Right to object: You have the right to object to the processing of all or part of your personal data (e.g., for direct marketing).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data—including utilizing secure Google infrastructure for storage—we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
