Branlly: Turning an Event Business Into a 24/7 Booking Machine

How a Nairobi event management company went from handling every inquiry by phone to running a full digital platform with a client dashboard, automated bookings, and an SEO-optimised presence that works while they sleep.

Client
Branlly
Industry
Event Management
Location
Nairobi, Kenya
Status
Live · Own Domain

01 — The ProblemEvery Booking Lived in Someone's Phone

Before the platform, Branlly's entire booking pipeline ran on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and memory. Inquiries came in at all hours, event details lived in scattered chats, and every missed call was potentially a missed client. There was no central place where a prospective client could see services, check credibility, and commit — the business was invisible outside its immediate network.

The brief was clear: Branlly needed a digital presence that didn't just describe the business, but actively operated it — capturing bookings, organising client information, and building trust with people who had never heard of them.

02 — The ApproachA Platform, Not a Brochure

Rather than a static showcase site, we designed Branlly as a working platform with three connected layers:

  • Public-facing site — an SEO-optimised web presence presenting services, past events, and social proof, structured to rank for event-management searches in Nairobi.
  • Booking system — a structured intake flow that captures event type, date, scale, and budget, replacing the back-and-forth of phone screening.
  • Client dashboard — a private area where clients track their event details and status, cutting the "just checking in" calls dramatically.
"The site had to do the talking, the qualifying, and the organising — so the team could focus on delivering great events."

The stack was chosen for reliability and maintainability: a database-driven backend for bookings and client records, and a fast, mobile-first frontend, since most of Branlly's audience discovers them on a phone.

03 — The OutcomeBookings Without the Phone Tag

Branlly now runs on its own domain as a live, fully operational platform. Inquiries arrive structured and pre-qualified, client communication happens in one place, and the business has a professional footprint it can point any prospective client to.
